How to play

Solve five crossword-style clues, bank golden letters, then weave one golden thread through the board.

  1. Each of the five rounds presents a crossword-style clue for a five-letter word. Find it above the letter grid, directly below the “Word X of 5 · 5 letters” round label.
  2. Press a tile and drag through four more letters. Move only up, down, left, or right; drag back over the previous tile to erase a step. Directional arrows show the route, and releasing submits it.
  3. Green means the right letter in the right spot, gold means the right letter in another spot, and gray means the letter is absent. You get six guesses per clue.
  4. Win a round and one scattered finale letter pops gold and banks into the tray. Then the board locks in that word’s one fixed true trail as permanent walls — it may differ from the route you traced. Golden tiles always stay open.
  5. Every board is verified up front: each later answer has a route around earlier true trails, and a simple Golden Thread remains possible after every trail is sealed.
  6. After round five, the Golden Thread begins automatically. All five scattered gold positions appear; banked tiles show letters and missed letters stay hidden as “?”. The Gold Letters tray keeps the letters in the order you banked them, not spelling order — anagram the known letters to work out the finale. Other open cells are blank white, while sealed trails are solid black.
  7. For the Golden Thread, press and drag the shortest continuous route you can through every gold tile in spelling order. Gold tiles do not need to be adjacent — the thread may travel through any open cells between them. Never revisit a cell or enter a black obstacle — one simple path, pure Zip. Releasing after all five gold visits submits the thread.
  8. You get six thread attempts per entry. After a miss, gold tiles visited in the correct sequence position turn green and the others gray. Use all six and the finale locks until another golden letter is banked.
  9. Scoring: 100 points for each clue round won, −10 for each hint used, +50 for every golden letter still unbanked when you crack the finale, and a 600-point time bonus that drops by one point each second. The shortest possible Golden Thread is par: match it exactly to earn a flat +10 shortest-path bonus. Any longer winning thread earns 0 for this bonus.
