How to play

Trace words, bank gold, thread the finale.

  • 5 rounds, each with a clue for a 5-letter word.
  • Drag through adjacent tiles to trace your guess.
  • Green = right spot, gold = wrong spot, gray = not in word.
  • 6 guesses per round.
  • Win to bank a gold letter for the finale.
  • Finale: thread all 5 golds in order, no revisits, 6 tries.
  • Skip costs 25. Each hint costs 5.
